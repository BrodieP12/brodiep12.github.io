GiftListApp Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Welcome to GiftListApp. This Privacy Policy outlines how your personal information is handled when you use the GiftListApp mobile application and related services.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: When you register an account, we collect your email address and optional profile display name.
  • User-Generated Content: Gift lists, list item names, descriptions, external links, photos, reservation claims, and chat messages you create within the app.
  • Camera and Photo Library: With your permission, GiftListApp may access your device camera or photo library so you can take and upload gift photos.
  • Diagnostics & Analytics: Anonymous application telemetry, crash logs, and performance metrics to help us diagnose issues and ensure app stability.

2. How We Use Information

We use the collected information solely to:

  • Provide core list sharing, gift coordination, item claim tracking, and messaging features.
  • Synchronize your gift lists across devices and with friends you explicitly invite.
  • Deliver customer support, respond to user inquiries, and improve application reliability.

3. Cloud Infrastructure & Third-Party Services

GiftListApp utilizes secure cloud infrastructure (including Supabase for authentication, PostgreSQL database, and cloud storage, as well as PostHog for crash and analytics monitoring) to host application data. We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

4. Account & Data Deletion

Request Data Deletion

You have the full right to request deletion of your account and all associated personal data at any time without needing the app installed.

Visit our dedicated Account & Data Deletion Page or email giftlistapp.support@gmail.com with the subject line GiftListApp - Account and Data Deletion Request. All associated data will be permanently purged within 30 days.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact developer support at:
Email: giftlistapp.support@gmail.com
