GiftListApp Account & Data Policy

Account & Data Deletion Request

Last updated: November 21, 2026

At GiftListApp, we respect your privacy and provide a straightforward way for you to permanently delete your user account and all associated data stored on our servers.

How to Request Account & Data Deletion

You do not need to have the app installed on your device to request deletion. You can submit a deletion request anytime by following either of these steps:

Option 1: Quick Email Request

Click the button below to launch your email client with a pre-filled request:

Request Account Deletion via Email

Option 2: Manual Email Submission

Send an email directly to developer support:

  • Recipient: giftlistapp.support@gmail.com
  • Subject: GiftListApp - Account and Data Deletion Request
  • Content: Provide the email address and username registered with your GiftListApp account.

What Data Will Be Deleted

When your deletion request is processed, the following data is permanently purged from our database and cloud storage:

  • User Profile: Your account identifier, email address, display name, and avatar picture.
  • Gift Lists: All gift lists created by you, including titles, descriptions, occasions, and custom sorting.
  • List Items: All gift items, notes, store links, estimated prices, and claimed status.
  • Claim & Reservation History: Records of any items you have claimed on friends' lists.
  • Uploaded Media: All gift photos and item pictures stored in cloud storage.
  • Chat Messages & Conversations: Direct message history and conversations associated with your account.
  • Friendships & Connections: All friend connections and contact links.

Data Retention and Processing Time

  • Processing Time: Account deletion requests are verified and completed within 30 calendar days of receipt.
  • Retention Policy: Once processed, no personal data, list content, or uploaded photos are retained. Transient system logs are purged automatically in accordance with standard data hygiene practices.
